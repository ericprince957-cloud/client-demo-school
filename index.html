Frequently asked questions

What ages do you take?

We accept children from age 2 (nursery) through to primary 6 (age 11). We place each child in the class that fits their age and readiness.

Can I visit before enrolling?

Yes — we encourage it. Book a visit by WhatsApp or phone and we will show you around the school on a weekday during school hours.

How do I get fee information?

Send us a message on WhatsApp or call the school office. We will share the current term fees and payment options with you directly.

What are the school hours?

School runs Monday to Friday. Drop-off is from 7:30 am and pick-up is by 2:00 pm. After-school care is available until 6:00 pm.
